Infrastructure proof

522 of 522 participant pods ready and 667 of 667 routes admitted at snapshot. Eight shared model replicas were ready: five Granite 3.2 8B Tools, two Granite 2B via OVMS, and one Nomic embedding endpoint.

Operational pivot

The original interpretation was 90 seat-environments. Live clarification required 270. Flightpath moved from DR candidate to emergency execution service for the additional 180 seats.

Measured gaps

16 defects/performance findings were recorded: 11 severity S1 and 5 S2. Five were mitigated during the pilot; mitigation is not durable verification. Journey completion and per-seat inference usage remain incomplete.
