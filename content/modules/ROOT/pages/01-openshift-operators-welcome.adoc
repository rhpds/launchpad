== Verify access

. Open the *Terminal* tab.
. Run:
+
[source,bash,role=execute,subs=attributes+]
----
oc project {namespace}
oc auth can-i create deployments.apps -n {namespace}
oc get pods -n {namespace}
----
. Confirm the authorization result is `yes` and the active project is your assigned namespace.

The *OpenShift Console* tab opens the {cluster_display_name} console. If
OpenShift asks for an identity provider, choose *launchpad-public*. Your current
participant session signs you in without another username or password, and
Launchpad limits access to `{namespace}`.
