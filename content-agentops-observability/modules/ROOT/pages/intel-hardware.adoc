== Why Intel for AI inference?

* **Purpose-built for AI**: Intel's AI hardware is designed for deep learning training and inference workloads
* **High throughput**: vLLM batches concurrent model requests efficiently
* **Scalable**: Launchpad reserves aggregate capacity before creating workshop seats
* **Evidence-driven**: The catalog publishes only the seat count proven by live certification
* **Enterprise-ready**: Integrated with Red Hat OpenShift AI for deployment, observability, and lifecycle management
